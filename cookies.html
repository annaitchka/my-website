Chocolate chip cookies

Ingredients

  • 140g butter, softened
  • 140g brown sugar (soft)
  • 100g white sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 3/4 tsp baking soda
  • 3/4 tsp flaky sea salt (Maldon preferred)
  • 250 g all purpose flour
  • 180g chocolate (dark or milk or mix), chopped into chunks or chips

Method

  1. Put the butter and both sugars in a stand mixer or mixing bowl. Cream together using a paddle attachment, a hand-held electric whisk or a wooden spoon for 1–2 minutes until paler but not fluffy. (Do not mix for too long, in this case you don’t want the mixture to be super light and fluffy as that will cause the cookie to deflate later when cooking.)
  2. Add the egg and beat until evenly combined
  3. mix dry Ingredients in a separate bowl
  4. fold in dry ingredients into butter, sugar, egg mixture
  5. add the chopped chocolate and fold into mix
  6. Scoop ~60g portions of dough, roll into balls and place on parchment lined baking sheets
  7. Chill for at least 1 hour in fridge
  8. Preheat oven to 180C
  9. Spread dough balls evenly as they will spread. Bake the cookies for at least 12 minutes. You want the middle to still be underset
  10. Allow cookies to cool on baking set and then enjoy!
